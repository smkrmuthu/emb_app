Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Explain My Bill (Phase 1)

Explain My Bill reads a photo or PDF of your bill and explains it in plain language, checking it against Indian GST and consumer-protection rules. This page explains what happens to what you upload.

In short: your bill photo/PDF is sent to be read, is not stored on our servers afterward, and no account or personal profile is created. It is shared with one third party — Anthropic — solely to read the bill's contents.

What we collect

When you scan a bill, the photo or PDF you provide is sent to our backend (a Cloudflare Worker) and forwarded to Anthropic's Claude API to be read into structured data (biller name, amounts, line items, dates). We do not require or collect your name, email, phone number, or any account information — there are no user accounts in this version of the app.

What we don't do

  • We do not store your bill photos or PDFs on our servers after they've been read.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not require sign-in or a user account.

Third-party processing

Bill content is sent to Anthropic, whose API reads the bill and returns structured data. Anthropic processes this data under its own privacy policy and API terms. No other third party receives your bill content.

Data retention

Scan results (the decoded bill shown in the app) live only in the app's local session — they are not saved to any server-side database, and are cleared when you reload or close the app. Nothing persists between visits yet.

Your bill's sensitive details

Please avoid uploading bills that contain full unmasked financial account numbers or government ID numbers where avoidable. Credit card statements are read for the amounts and dates needed to explain EMI/interest charges; masked card numbers (e.g. "XXXX 5323") may be read as printed but are not used for anything beyond display within your own decoded result.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, when accounts or persistent storage are introduced in a later phase — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's store page.
